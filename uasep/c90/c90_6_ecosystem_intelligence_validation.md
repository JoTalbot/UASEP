# C90.6 Ecosystem Intelligence Validation

## Validation matrix
- ecosystem snapshots preserve provenance and freshness
- stale observations are detected
- conflicting observations remain explicit
- capability discovery cannot expand privileges
- dependency failures are surfaced before execution
- impact analysis identifies affected systems
- high-risk actions require authorization
- policy violations block side effects
- audit records capture intelligence decisions
- degraded integrations remain isolated

## Exit criteria
C90 is complete when ecosystem intelligence is observable, bounded, policy-aware, testable, and unable to bypass authorization or governance controls.
