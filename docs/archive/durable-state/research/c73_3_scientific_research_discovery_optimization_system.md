# C73.3 Scientific Research, Discovery & Innovation Optimization System

## Purpose
Optimize research workflows without compromising evidence quality, safety or reproducibility.

## Functions
- experiment prioritization
- resource allocation
- research pipeline optimization
- duplicate effort reduction
- uncertainty analysis
- validation coverage improvement
- innovation impact assessment

## Constraints
Optimization ≠ removing validation
Speed ≠ scientific correctness
Metrics ≠ complete evidence
