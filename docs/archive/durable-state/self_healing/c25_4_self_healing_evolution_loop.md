# C25.4 Self-Healing Evolution Loop

## Purpose
Autonomous evolution cycle for self-healing capabilities.

## Capabilities

- analysis of recovery effectiveness
- improvement of healing strategies
- prevention of repeated failures
- integration of operational feedback
- controlled resilience evolution
- governance-aware updates

## Pipeline

Fault History
↓
Recovery Analysis
↓
Improvement Proposal
↓
Validation
↓
Self-Healing Update
↓
Monitoring
↓
Feedback Loop

## States

- OBSERVED
- ANALYZING
- EVOLVING
- VALIDATING
- UPDATED
- ROLLBACK_REQUIRED

## Integration

- Fault Detection Intelligence
