# C63.3 Decision Optimization System

## Purpose
Improve decision quality through evaluation of alternatives, risks, evidence and outcomes without weakening governance.

## Capabilities
- alternative ranking
- trade-off analysis
- risk-adjusted optimization
- scenario comparison
- bias and drift detection
- decision quality metrics

## Constraints
Optimization cannot override authority, safety, security, compliance or evidence requirements.

## Metrics
- decision accuracy
- outcome quality
- uncertainty calibration
- risk coverage
- regret reduction
- reproducibility
