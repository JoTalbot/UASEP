# C63.3 Autonomous Decision Intelligence Optimization System

## Purpose
Optimize decision quality, consistency, speed and clarity without weakening evidence, uncertainty, governance or authority boundaries.

## Capabilities
- alternative ranking optimization
- decision quality analysis
- uncertainty reduction
- bias detection improvement
- explanation quality improvement
- review efficiency optimization
- decision outcome comparison
- controlled experimentation

## Constraints
- Optimization cannot create authority.
- Confidence cannot replace approval.
- Metrics cannot redefine correctness.
- Evidence cannot be discarded for score improvement.

## Completion criterion
Decision optimization improves outcomes while preserving governance.
