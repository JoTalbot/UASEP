# C62.3 Simulation Optimization System

## Purpose
Optimize simulation quality, scenario coverage, prediction reliability and experiment efficiency without weakening validation or uncertainty controls.

## Capabilities
- scenario prioritization
- model efficiency optimization
- simulation resource allocation
- prediction quality improvement
- experiment scheduling
- uncertainty reduction
- coverage optimization
- validation optimization

## Constraints
- Optimization cannot hide uncertainty.
- Faster simulation cannot replace validation.
- Predictions cannot become permissions.
- Safety and governance remain mandatory.

## Metrics
- prediction accuracy
- scenario coverage
- simulation cost
- validation quality
- uncertainty calibration
- reproducibility
