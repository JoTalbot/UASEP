# C53.3 Autonomous Strategy & Policy Optimization System

## Purpose
Optimize UASEP strategies and policies for objective alignment, risk coverage, consistency, measurable impact and governance quality while preserving authority boundaries, evidence, provenance, uncertainty, safety, privacy and compliance constraints.

## Capabilities
- strategy portfolio optimization
- policy portfolio optimization
- objective alignment analysis
- policy conflict and precedence optimization
- risk/mitigation optimization
- scenario and impact optimization
- stakeholder/authority alignment
- alternative strategy comparison
- policy exception minimization and quality analysis
- review cadence optimization
- controlled promotion and rollback

## Optimization flow
```text
Strategy / Policy Portfolio
    -> Objective / Constraint / Authority Analysis
    -> Risk / Conflict / Impact / Cost Analysis
    -> Gap / Redundancy / Drift Detection
    -> Candidate Improvements
    -> Scenario / Simulation / Replay
    -> Governance + Security + Compliance Review
    -> Baseline Comparison
    -> Controlled Promotion
    -> Monitoring
    -> Outcome Feedback
```

## Objectives
Optimize, as applicable:
- objective alignment
- risk coverage and residual risk
- policy consistency
- authority clarity
- expected impact
- alternative coverage
- exception quality
- implementation feasibility
- review efficiency
- policy/strategy maintenance cost

High-impact safety, security, privacy, integrity and compliance objectives take precedence over convenience or cost reduction.

## Candidate evaluation
Candidates are evaluated against versioned baselines using representative scenarios, historical outcomes, conflict tests, impact analysis, simulation/replay and controlled workloads. Evaluation must account for hidden authority conflicts, policy gaps, unintended incentives, cascading effects and weakened safeguards.

## Hard constraints
1. Objectives cannot be silently weakened to improve optimization scores.
2. Safety, security, privacy and compliance controls cannot be removed for efficiency.
3. Authority boundaries cannot be broadened implicitly.
4. Conflicting policies remain visible until explicitly resolved.
5. Unknown impacts remain explicit.
6. Policy exceptions cannot become unrestricted defaults through optimization.
7. Optimization cannot self-authorize enforcement or execution.
8. High-impact changes retain required approval/escalation.
9. Every promoted change has monitoring and rollback conditions.

## Completion criterion
The strategy and policy optimizer is ready when strategy alignment, policy consistency, risk coverage, authority clarity, scenario impact, alternatives and exceptions can be improved against measurable baselines without weakening governance, safety, security, privacy, compliance, provenance or authorization boundaries.
