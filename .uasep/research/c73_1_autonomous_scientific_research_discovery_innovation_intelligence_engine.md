# C73.1 Autonomous Scientific Research, Discovery & Innovation Intelligence Engine

## Purpose
Enable governed scientific discovery workflows through hypothesis generation, evidence analysis, experimentation support and innovation tracking while preserving verification, provenance and safety boundaries.

## Capabilities
- hypothesis generation
- literature and knowledge synthesis
- experiment planning support
- evidence evaluation
- discovery tracking
- innovation opportunity analysis
- uncertainty management
- reproducibility support
- provenance preservation

## Flow
Observation -> Knowledge -> Hypothesis -> Experiment -> Validation -> Discovery Record -> Feedback

## Boundaries
Research ≠ Truth without validation
Hypothesis ≠ Fact
Discovery ≠ Automatic Deployment
Innovation ≠ Safety Override

## Completion
Scientific intelligence operates as a governed discovery assistant with evidence, validation and provenance controls.
