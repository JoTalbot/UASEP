# C73.2 Scientific Research, Discovery & Innovation Framework

## Purpose
Provide governance structure for scientific workflows, evidence handling and innovation lifecycle management.

## Components
- research identity
- evidence registry
- hypothesis lifecycle
- experiment governance
- reproducibility controls
- peer validation support
- discovery archive
- innovation review

## States
IDEA -> HYPOTHESIS -> EXPERIMENT -> VALIDATION -> VERIFIED -> ARCHIVED

## Rules
Evidence requires provenance.
Validation is independent from generation.
Innovation requires controlled evaluation.
