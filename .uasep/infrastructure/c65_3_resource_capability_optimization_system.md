# C65.3 Resource Capability Optimization System

## Purpose
Optimize infrastructure utilization, capability availability and resource efficiency within approved security, policy and authority boundaries.

## Capabilities
- capacity optimization
- scheduling improvement
- allocation efficiency
- bottleneck detection
- redundancy analysis
- cost efficiency analysis
- resilience improvement

## Constraints
- Optimization cannot expand authority.
- Security and safety constraints are mandatory.
- Resource changes require validation and audit.
- Efficiency cannot override reliability.
