# C12.2 Decision Confidence System

## Purpose
System for evaluating confidence, uncertainty and risk of autonomous decisions.

## Capabilities

- Decision confidence scoring
- Evidence quality evaluation
- Risk estimation
- Uncertainty tracking
- Human review triggers
- Integration with governance and audit layers

## Pipeline

Decision Input
↓
Evidence Analysis
↓
Confidence Calculation
↓
Risk Evaluation
↓
Review Decision
↓
Decision Memory Update

## States

- PENDING
- ANALYZING
- CONFIDENT
- LOW_CONFIDENCE
- REVIEW_REQUIRED
- VERIFIED
- UNKNOWN
