# C74.1 Autonomous Environmental, Physical World & Embodied Intelligence Engine

## Purpose
Coordinate physical-world awareness, environmental interaction and embodied intelligence with safety, verification, provenance and authorization boundaries.

## Capabilities
- environment state modeling
- physical context understanding
- sensor and actuator coordination
- embodied interaction planning
- risk-aware adaptation
- simulation-to-reality validation
- physical feedback integration

## Principle
Embodiment provides interaction capability, not unrestricted authority.
