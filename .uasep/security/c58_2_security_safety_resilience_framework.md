# C58.2 Autonomous Security, Safety & Resilience Framework

## Purpose
Define auditable structures for security controls, safety constraints, resilience policies and incident lifecycle management.

## Components
- identity and access controls
- threat models
- safety policies
- resilience objectives
- incident records
- recovery procedures
- verification requirements
- audit provenance

## Lifecycle
```text
IDENTIFIED -> ASSESSED -> CONTROLLED -> VERIFIED -> MONITORED -> IMPROVED
```

## Rules
1. Security, safety and resilience controls are mandatory boundaries.
2. High-impact risks require escalation.
3. Recovery actions remain authorized and scoped.
4. Evidence and incident history are preserved.
5. Controls are versioned and reproducible.

## Completion
The framework enables governed protection, recovery and continuous hardening without authority expansion.
