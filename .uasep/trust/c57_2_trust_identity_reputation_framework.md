# C57.2 Autonomous Trust, Identity & Reputation Framework

## Purpose
Define auditable structures for identities, capabilities, trust evidence, reputation records and governed trust decisions.

## Components
- identity registry
- capability registry
- trust evidence records
- reputation history
- verification records
- trust decision records
- privacy and retention controls
- audit trail

## Trust record
Includes:
- identity reference
- verification status
- capability scope
- evidence references
- reputation events
- confidence level
- limitations
- expiration/review conditions
- decision history

## Lifecycle
`CLAIMED -> VERIFIED -> ASSESSED -> TRUSTED -> REVIEWED -> EXPIRED/REVOKED`

## Rules
- Trust is evidence-based.
- Reputation is contextual, not absolute.
- Authorization remains separate.
- Conflicts and uncertainty remain visible.

## Completion criterion
Trust infrastructure is ready when identities and reputation can be represented, reviewed and audited with explicit evidence and boundaries.
