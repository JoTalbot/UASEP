# C26.4 Knowledge Evolution Loop

## Purpose

Autonomous evolution cycle for continuous improvement of the knowledge layer.

## Capabilities

- knowledge lifecycle analysis
- discovery of improvement opportunities
- adaptive knowledge updates
- learning feedback integration
- controlled knowledge evolution
- governance validation

Knowledge Metrics
↓

↓
Improvement Proposal
↓
Validation
↓

↓
Monitoring
↓
Feedback Loop
