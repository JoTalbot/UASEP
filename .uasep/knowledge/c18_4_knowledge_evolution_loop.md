# C18.4 Knowledge Evolution Loop

## Purpose

Knowledge evolution cycle for continuous improvement of the UASEP knowledge layer.

## Capabilities

- knowledge lifecycle management
- knowledge updates
- relevance evaluation
- outdated knowledge detection
- feedback-driven improvement
- controlled knowledge evolution
- audit tracking

## Pipeline

Knowledge State
↓
Evolution Analysis
↓
Improvement Proposal
↓
Validation
↓
Knowledge Update
↓
Monitoring
↓
Feedback Integration

## States

- OBSERVED
- ANALYZING
- PROPOSED
- VALIDATING
- UPDATED
- ROLLBACK_REQUIRED

## Integration

- Knowledge Acquisition Engine
- Knowledge Validation System
- Knowledge Graph Intelligence
- Decision Intelligence
- Evolution Governance
