# C7.4 Governance Decision Records

## Purpose
Maintain traceable records of governance decisions.

## Capabilities

- Decision history storage
- Reason and context capture
- Evidence linkage
- Approval tracking
- Impact recording
- Future review support

## Decision Flow

Request
→ Analysis
→ Evidence Review
→ Governance Decision
→ Record Creation
→ Knowledge Update

## Decision States

- PROPOSED
- APPROVED
- REJECTED
- SUPERSEDED
- UNKNOWN
